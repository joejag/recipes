# ingredients

- 400g spaghetti
- 1T olive oil
- 200g pancetta
- 2 garlic cloves
- 3 eggs
- 75ml double cream
- 50g parmesan

# Technique

1. Cook pasta
2. Oil in medium pan, cook pancetta with garlic. Remove from heat when fat melted
3. Mix eggs, cream, parmesan & season
4. Mix mixtures with pasta
