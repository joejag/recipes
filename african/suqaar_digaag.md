# Suqaar Digaag

Xawaash blend

- 2t ground cumin,
- 0.5t ground coriander,
- 0.5t ground turmeric,
- 0.5 smoked paprika
- 0.25t ground black pepper
- 0.10t ground cloves,
- 0.10t ground cinnamon
- 2 cardamom, ground
- 1t salt

Others

- onion, oo, chicken, lime juice, garlic, peppers, coriander, rice

## Recipe

- Toast spices on low for 1m, remove.
- Pan on med heat, add 2T oo, onion for 7m
- Combine chicken with all but 1t of Xawaash in bowl
- Add 2T garlic for 2m
- Add chicken, heat to med-high until cooked
- Add red & yellow bell pepper with 1t of Xawaash blend. Turn heat low, 5m
- Add coriander leaf, 2T lime juice. Serve with rice

## Pilaf Rice

- 2T oo, onion + 2t garlic on med-high for 4m
- Add spices (0.5t coriander, 0.5t paprika, 0.25 turmeric), cook for 5m
- Add rice, mix, add water (1.25x rice). Boil, then low, cook covered for 15-20m.
