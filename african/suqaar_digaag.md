# Suqaar Digaag

Xawaash blend

- 2t ground cumin,
- 0.5t ground coriander,
- 0.5t ground turmeric,
- 0.5 smoked paprika
- 0.25t ground black pepper
- 0.10t ground cloves,
- 0.10t ground cinnamon
- 2 cardamom, ground
- 1t salt

Others

- onion, oo, chicken, lemon juice, garlic, jalapeno, peppers, coriander, rice

## Recipe

- Toast spices on low for 1m, remove.
- Pan on med heat, add 2T oo, onion for 7m
- Combine chicken, 1.5T lemon juice with all but 1t of Xawaash in bowl
- Add 2T garlic, jalapeno for 2m
- Add chicken, heat to med-high until cooked
- Add red & yellow bell pepper with 1t of Xawaash blend and coriander leaf. Turn heat low, 10m
- Serve with rice
